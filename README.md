# 🧭 CampusCompass - Smart College Explorer & Freshers' Companion

An interactive, responsive web application built for college freshers and campus visitors to explore buildings, food courts, clubs, survival hacks, and get instant answers from an AI peer mentor.

## 🚀 Live Local Demo
The app is running at: **https://campus-compass-fresherssss.vercel.app/
---

## ✨ Features Built for Hackathon Judges

1. **Dual Audience Persona Switcher**:
   - 🎒 **Fresher Mode**: Prioritizes study sanctuaries, affordable meals under ₹60, club recruitment alerts, and survival FAQs.
   - 🏛️ **Visitor Mode**: Highlights architectural spots, guest cafeterias, innovation centers, and walking tours.

2. **Campus Spots & Labs Directory**:
   - Filter by *Study & Libraries*, *Labs & Tech*, *Hangouts & Chill*, *Sports & Gym*, and *Admin & Services*.
   - Filter by campus zones (*North Tech Zone*, *Central Hub*, *South Sports Hub*, etc.).
   - Verified **"Senior Insider Tips"** on every card (e.g. quiet corner desks, best charging sockets).

3. **Food Radar (Canteens & Eateries)**:
   - Live crowd rush indicators (*Low Rush*, *Moderate*, *Night Rush*).
   - Budget filters (`₹` < ₹60, `₹₹` ₹60–₹150, `₹₹₹`).
   - "Must-Try" signature items with exact prices.

4. **Clubs & Activities Hub**:
   - Technical, Cultural, Arts, Entrepreneurship (E-Cell), and Sports societies.
   - Upcoming flagship hackathons and events with dates, prize pools, and 1-click RSVP.

5. **Interactive Campus Grid & Wayfinding**:
   - Visual campus coordinate map with clickable interactive pins.
   - Real-time side preview with walking route estimations (e.g. *~4 mins walk from Main Gate*).

6. **Senior Survival Kit & FAQs**:
   - Wi-Fi login guide, low-cost printing shops, hostel curfews, attendance policies, and campus Dos and Don'ts.
   - 1-click copy for 24/7 Medical, Security, and Anti-Ragging helplines.

7. **Interactive "Senior AI" Assistant**:
   - In-app chatbot answering queries like *"Where can I get good cold coffee?"*, *"How to connect to wifi?"*, or *"Which tech clubs can freshers join?"*.

8. **"My Tour" / Custom Checklist Planner**:
   - Bookmark any spot or eatery with persistent local storage saving.
   - Interactive checkbox itinerary to tick off places on day one.

9. **Project List & Import History Manager**:
   - View, switch, and save multiple campus project configurations with timestamps and spot tallies.
   - 1-click restore or switch between saved campus versions.
   - Save custom campus snapshots directly to Project History.

10. **Universal File Import & Export**:
    - Import full university setups or custom spots from `.json` and `.csv` files.
    - Export complete JSON backups or spreadsheet CSVs.
    - Preloaded sample datasets in `public/samples/`.
